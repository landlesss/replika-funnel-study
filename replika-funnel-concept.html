01 / Why this experiment

A specific observation.
A modest hypothesis.

I reviewed the public desktop homepage, not Replika's ad traffic or internal data. In that session, “Get started” opened an app-download QR panel. That made me curious about what a visitor could experience before the handoff.

Observed

The first action is a handoff.

The public desktop CTA led to a QR download panel. A cookie dialog was also visible, obscuring part of its instructions. This is one session, not evidence of a conversion problem.

Hypothesis

Show a little of the feeling.

A short preview may help visitors understand what they are installing. It could also add friction. Both outcomes are plausible, which is why this deserves a test.

Deliberate constraint

No emotional quiz. No pressure.

Use fictional, low-stakes prompts. No mental-health profiling, fabricated testimonials or urgency. The preview is labelled as scripted, and the next step stays optional.

02 / How I'd test it

A click isn't
a new relationship.

Randomize eligible visitors into A or B with stable assignment. Keep traffic source, offer and handoff destination consistent. Agree the baseline, minimum meaningful effect and sample size before launch.

Preferred primary outcome

First app conversation
per eligible visitor.

Only if consented cross-device attribution can support it. Otherwise, call handoff clicks a proxy and report that limitation.

Watch the whole journey.

Preview completion → handoff action → app onboarding → first conversation. Follow subscription conversion and retention where measurable.

Guardrails: error rate, mobile speed, accessibility, consent usability and downstream user experience.

No uplift claimed. No experiment has run. A QR panel opening does not mean a QR scan, install or purchase.
